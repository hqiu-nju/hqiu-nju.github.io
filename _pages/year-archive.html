Beyond the telescope

Two places. A shared sky.

I spent my early childhood in Adelaide before moving with my parents to Nanjing, where I completed high school and my undergraduate education. I’m a fluent bilingual speaker and lucky to call both Australia and China home.

I hope to help build deeper ties and friendships between the two countries. My Chinese name is 邱昊 — Qiu (qiū), the family name, comes first, followed by Hao (hào). You can also call me Harry.
